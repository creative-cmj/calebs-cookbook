5 CROWNS · TENDER, NOT HARD

Garlic Butter
Broccoli

Steam it first, then finish it in buttery garlic. Soft inside, bright green outside.

⏱ 10–12 min🥦 12–15 servings🍳 2 batches or 2 skillets

Ingredient checklist

STEAM TIMER

06:00

Covered: steam until fork-tender.

Cook it

1

Cut it small. Cut florets into bite-size pieces. Peel the tough outside of the stem, then thinly slice the tender inside.

2

Steam in batches. Divide the broccoli between 2 batches or 2 skillets. Use about ½ cup water per batch, plus salt and pepper. Cover and cook on medium for 5–7 minutes.

3

Test it. Poke the thickest stem with a fork. It should go in easily. If still hard, cover for another 1–2 minutes.

4

Add garlic butter. Remove lid and let leftover water cook off. Turn heat to medium-low, add butter and garlic, and stir for 1–2 minutes.

5

Finish. Add a squeeze of lemon or Parmesan if you want. Serve right away.

Important: Add garlic only at the end. It stays tasty instead of burning while the broccoli gets tender.
