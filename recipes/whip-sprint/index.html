THE EASY VERSION

Cold cream + soft peaks.

Use cold heavy whipping cream and stop when it holds a soft, fluffy peak. Whip a little longer only if you want it stiffer.

Check now: it should be fluffy and hold soft peaks. Stop before it turns grainy.
1

Chill your setup

Put the bowl and whisk or mixer beaters in the freezer for 10 minutes if you can. Keep the heavy cream cold.

2

Add everything

Pour in 1 cup cold heavy whipping cream. Add 2 tbsp powdered sugar and 1 tsp vanilla.

3

Whip to soft peaks

Whisk hard or use a mixer on medium speed. When you lift the whisk, the peak should curl over at the tip. Start the check timer.

4

Choose the finish

For soft whipped cream, stop at soft peaks. For piping or a sturdier topping, whip 10–20 seconds more until peaks stand up. Do not overwhip—grainy cream is on its way to butter.

5

Serve or chill

Spoon it on right away, or cover and refrigerate until serving. Give it a quick stir if it sits for a while.

Make it extra good

For a richer, more stable topping, use powdered sugar instead of regular sugar. Add a tiny pinch of salt if you want the vanilla flavor to pop.
