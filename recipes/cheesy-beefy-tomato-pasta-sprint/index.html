Cheesy Beefy Tomato Pasta

Family dinner for 4, plus good leftovers for tomorrow.

Use 1 large can of beef (or 2 smaller cans). This recipe is meant to be saucy, cheesy, and filling—not a random pantry mix.

Cook it

1
Boil 1 lb pasta in salted water until just tender. Before draining, save ½ cup pasta water.
2
Optional: cook the chopped yellow bell pepper in 1 tbsp oil for 4–5 minutes.
3
Add the canned beef, 2 cans tomato sauce, 2–3 tbsp tomato paste, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, black pepper, and a tiny pinch of sugar.
4
Simmer the sauce for 8–10 minutes. Stir in a splash of pasta water if it gets too thick.
5
Toss in pasta. Top with 2 cups mozzarella, cover 2–3 minutes, and let it melt. Taste before adding salt.

Make it taste even better

  • Red-pepper flakes for a little kick.
  • Stir in 1 tbsp butter at the end for richer sauce.
  • Use Parmesan if you have it.
  • Too thick: add pasta water. Too tomato-y: add a tiny pinch more sugar.
