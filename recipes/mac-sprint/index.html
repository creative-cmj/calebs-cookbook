1

Make the roux

Melt 2 tbsp butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 3½ tbsp flour and keep whisking for 2 minutes.

2

Make the sauce

Slowly add 1½ cups whole milk while whisking constantly. Cook about 10 minutes, stirring often, until thickened.

3

Melt the sauce cheese

For the smoothest sauce, grate cheese from blocks if you can. Take the pan off heat, then stir in 7 oz sharp white cheddar, 1 oz pepper jack, salt, chipotle chili powder, garlic powder, and black pepper until fully melted. Do not boil the cheese sauce.

4

Prep pasta + dish

Heat oven to 350°F. Cook the macaroni according to its package, but drain it 1 minute before the shortest listed time. Rinse with cold water, then drain it really well. Oil the 8-inch dish.

5

Assemble with a crunchy top

Mix pasta and sauce until coated, then put it in the dish. Top with the extra white cheddar and pepper jack. Mix the 3 tbsp bread crumbs with 1 tbsp melted butter, sprinkle...[truncated]

6

Bake

Bake 20 minutes, until bubbling and browned around the edges. Start the timer when it enters the oven.

Spice switch

This is the spicy version. If you want it mild, replace the pepper jack with more white cheddar and skip the chipotle chili powder.
