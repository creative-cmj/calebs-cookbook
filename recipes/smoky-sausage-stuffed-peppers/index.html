Prep the peppers

Start at 400°F
Hot oven + raw meat: ask an adult for help with the oven, knives, and hot baking dishes if needed. Keep the raw-meat cutting board and tools away from the peppers and toppings.
1

Open and season the peppers

Heat the oven to 400°F. Cut the tops off all 10 peppers, remove the seeds and white ribs, then rub the outsides lightly with olive oil. Stand them in two baking dishes.

2

Par-bake for sweeter, softer peppers

Bake the empty peppers for 12 minutes. While they cook, make the filling. After this timer, lower the oven to 375°F.

Empty peppersReady
12:00

Build the flavorful filling

Brown hard, then layer flavor
3

Brown the meat and onion

Warm a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the beef, sausage, and onions until browned with no pink meat left. Drain excess grease, then stir in garlic for 30 seconds.

4

Bloom the tomato paste and spices

Add tomato paste, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, red-pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper. Stir for one full minute until the paste turns darker red and smells toasted.

5

Finish the filling

Stir in the fire-roasted tomatoes and half the marinara. Simmer 4 minutes so the filling stays thick, then fold in the rice and 2 cups of mozzarella. Taste and add salt if needed.

Fill, bake, and melt

Finish at 375°F
6

Fill and sauce

Spoon the filling into every par-baked pepper. Pour the remaining marinara around the peppers, not over their tops. Cover both dishes tightly with foil.

7

Covered bake

Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes. The peppers should be tender and the center of the filling should reach 160°F.

Covered bakeReady
25:00
8

Cheesy final bake

Uncover, divide the remaining 1½ cups mozzarella over the peppers, then bake until melty and lightly browned. Rest 5 minutes, then add basil or parsley if you have it.

Serving + leftovers

Built for tonight and tomorrow
PLATE ITServe each person one pepper with extra sauce from the pan. A simple salad, garlic bread, or roasted broccoli works well next to it.
LEFTOVERSCool within 2 hours. Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat until the filling is steaming hot.
