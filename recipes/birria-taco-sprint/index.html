Cook the birria

Keep the red broth!
Hot steam + oil: use oven mitts or a thick towel for the pot lid. Keep raw beef tools separate, then wash hands and surfaces before handling tortillas and toppings.
1

Season and brown the beef

Pat the chuck dry. Season with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large 8-quart heavy pot on medium-high, then brown the beef on all sides. Work in batches if needed.

2

Soften the dried chiles

Pull off stems and shake out most seeds from the guajillo and ancho chiles. Cover with hot water for 10 minutes.

Chile soak
10:00
3

Blend the red sauce

Blend softened chiles, onion, garlic, chipotle peppers, crushed tomatoes, beef broth, vinegar, cumin, oregano, and cinnamon until smooth. Carefully vent the blender lid because the mixture is hot.

4

Slow-cook until shreddable

Pour sauce over the browned beef and add bay leaves. Cover. Cook on low stovetop for 3½–4 hours, until the beef pulls apart easily with forks. Slow cooker: use an 8+ quart cooker (or split between two); low 8 hours or high 5–6 hours. Instant Pot: use an 8-quart pot, or cook in two batches in a 6-quart pot; pressure-cook each batch 60 minutes, then natural release.

Stovetop countdown
4:00:00
5

Shred and save the consommé

Move beef to a bowl and shred it. Taste the red broth and add salt if needed. Spoon some of the red oil from the top into a small bowl—this helps the tortillas get crispy.

Build crispy tacos

Make 2–3 at a time
6

Dip the tortilla

Warm a skillet on medium. Dip one corn tortilla lightly into the red oil on top of the consommé. Do not soak it until floppy.

7

Cheese, beef, fold, crisp

Lay tortilla in the skillet. Add cheese and shredded beef to one side, fold it over, and fry 2–3 minutes per side until crisp and melty.

Five-minute taco batch
5:00
8

Serve and dip

Top with diced onion, cilantro, and lime. Pour hot consommé into bowls and dip the tacos before each bite.
