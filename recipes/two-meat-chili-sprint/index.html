Two-Meat
Chili

Yes—using 1 lb ground beef + 1 lb Italian sausage makes the chili richer and more flavorful. The sausage brings seasoning, so start light on extra salt.

Why two meats?

Flavor move: brown both meats well, then toast the spices for one minute before adding tomatoes. That is what makes it taste deeper instead of plain.

Shopping + prep checklist

Cook it

1
Brown both meats.

Heat a big pot over medium-high. Add 1 lb beef and 1 lb sausage. Break them up small and cook until no pink remains. Drain off most of the grease.

2
Soften the vegetables.

Add onion and bell pepper. Cook 5–7 minutes until softened, then add garlic for 30 seconds.

3
Toast the spices.

Add chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and garlic powder. Stir constantly for 1 minute.

4
Add the chili base.

Stir in crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, drained beans, broth, brown sugar, and Worcestershire. Add 1 tsp black pepper and only 1 tsp salt to start.

5
Low simmer.

Bring to a gentle bubble, then turn to LOW with the lid cracked. Simmer 1½–2 hours, stirring every 10–15 minutes. Do not hard-boil it.

6
Taste and fix it.

Too thick: add broth or water. Too thin: simmer uncovered. Too tomato-y: add a small extra pinch of brown sugar. Add more salt or heat only after tasting.

Ready

Last things

Keep it safe: Use a clean spoon for tasting, wash hands after raw meat, and refrigerate the leftovers within 2 hours. Chili is usually even better tomorrow.
