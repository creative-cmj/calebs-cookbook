Make the lemon syrup

About 15 minutes
Best texture: Use a metal loaf pan or another shallow metal pan for faster, smoother freezing. Wash the lemons before zesting.
1

Dissolve the sugar

Add the sugar and water to a small pot. Warm over medium heat, stirring, just until the liquid turns clear and the sugar is fully dissolved. Do not boil it hard.

2

Steep the zest

Take the pot off the heat. Stir in the lemon zest and tiny pinch of salt. Let it sit so the zest flavors the syrup.

3

Add the fresh lemon

Strain out the zest if you want a perfectly smooth sorbet, then stir in all 1½ cups of lemon juice. Taste it: it should be bold and a little sweeter than you want the finished sorbet, since freezing dulls sweetness.

Chill the base

Do not skip
4

Get it cold first

Cover the lemon mixture and refrigerate until cold—at least 1 hour. A cold base freezes into smaller ice crystals, which makes the sorbet smoother.

Freeze, scrape, repeat

About 2½ hrs
5

First freeze

Pour the cold base into a shallow metal pan. Freeze it uncovered for 45 minutes, until the edges are icy and the center is slushy.

First freezer roundReady
45:00
6

Scrape it hard

Use a fork to scrape the frozen edges into the middle, then whisk or mash until the mixture looks like a thick lemon slush. Break up every icy chunk you can find.

7

Repeat three more times

Freeze, scrape, and stir again every 35 minutes. After the third repeat, it should be fluffy, smooth, and scoopable. If it gets too firm, let it sit on the counter 5–10 minutes before scooping.
