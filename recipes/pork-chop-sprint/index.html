Pan-Seared
Pork Chops

For 9 already-seasoned pork chops. Work in 3 batches so every chop gets a real brown crust.

3 batches of 3StovetopJuicy at 145°F

Quick plan

9seasoned chops
3chops per batch
145°Fsafe center temp

You already seasoned them—perfect. Do not add more salt. Just pat off any wet spots before they hit the hot pan.

Get ready

Cook the chops

1
Dry and heat.

Pat the 9 seasoned chops dry. Heat a heavy skillet on medium-high for 2–3 minutes, then add 1 tbsp oil.

2
Sear batch 1.

Put in 3 chops with space between them. Do not move them for 3½–4 minutes. Flip and cook 3–4 minutes more.

3
Butter-baste.

Lower heat to medium. Add 2 tbsp butter, 2 garlic cloves, and herbs. Spoon the foamy butter over the chops for about 1 minute.

4
Check temperature.

Take them out when the thickest part is 145°F. If they are thick and not ready, cook 1 more minute per side, then check again.

5
Repeat twice.

Wipe out burnt bits if needed. Use 1 tbsp oil and 2 tbsp butter for each new batch of 3.

6
Rest.

Rest all the chops on a plate for 5 minutes before cutting so the juice stays inside.

Ready
03:30

For thin chops, start checking early. For bone-in or thick chops, the thermometer decides—not the clock.

Last things

Keep it safe: Wash hands and the raw-meat plate. Use a clean plate for cooked chops. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
