& Meatballs

Built for your 1 lb ground beef + 1 lb Italian sausage. Make 24 medium meatballs, bake them, then finish them in sauce.

Sauce + pasta checklist

Cook it

1
Heat the oven and pan.

Set oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with foil or parchment, then lightly oil it.

2
Mix gently.

In a big bowl, combine beef, sausage, breadcrumbs, eggs, Parmesan, garlic, Italian seasoning, pepper, and milk if using. Mix just until combined—do not mash it hard.

3
Roll 24 meatballs.

Make golf-ball-sized meatballs, about 1⅓ oz each. Leave a little space between them on the pan.

4
Bake until cooked.

Bake for 18–22 minutes. Check the middle of a large one: it needs to reach 160°F.

5
Warm sauce and finish meatballs.

While they bake, warm 48 oz marinara in a large pot on low. Add cooked meatballs and simmer gently 10 minutes.

6
Boil spaghetti.

Boil salted water. Cook 24 oz spaghetti until just tender—usually 1 minute less than the box says. Save ½ cup pasta water before draining.

7
Toss and serve.

Toss spaghetti with a few scoops of sauce. Add a splash of pasta water if needed. Put meatballs and more sauce on top, then Parmesan if you want.

Last things

Keep it safe: Wash hands after raw meat, use a clean plate for cooked meatballs, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
