Cook it

1

Brown the beef. Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Cook 2.4 lb ground beef with the diced onion and optional bell pepper, breaking it apart, until no pink remains. Drain excess grease.

2

Add garlic. Stir in garlic and cook for 30 seconds.

3

Make the sauce. Add everything else except buns. Stir well, scraping up the flavorful browned bits.

4

Simmer. Bring it to a gentle bubble, lower to medium-low, then simmer 15–20 minutes uncovered. Stir every few minutes.

5

Serve. When it is thick enough to mound on a spoon, taste and adjust. Spoon onto toasted buns.

Quick fixes: Too sweet? Add 1 tsp vinegar. Too tangy? Add 1 tbsp brown sugar. Too thick? Add a splash of water. Too loose? Simmer longer.

Serving ideas

Top with pickles, sliced cheese, or extra mustard. Serve with corn, chips, fries, or a simple salad.
