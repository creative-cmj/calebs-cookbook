Sweet, buttery corn on the cob — the easy side for your ribs and your own special mac and cheese.

Ready in 10 min

BUTTERY BOILED CORN

Bright, sweet, and simple.

Boil the water first, cook the corn just long enough to heat it through, then finish with butter and your seasoning.

4–6ears of corn
5 minboil time
2 minrest + butter

What you need

4–6 ears
fresh corn, husked
4 tbsp
butter
½ tsp
salt, to taste
½ tsp
paprika
Optional
black pepper or chili powder
1 pot
water + tongs
Corn is ready. Drain it, add butter and seasoning, then serve while it’s hot.
CORN TIMER
5:00

Start it when the water is already boiling and you add the corn.

1

Get the water boiling

Fill a large pot with enough water to cover the corn. Bring it to a full boil.

2

Add the corn

Put in the husked corn. Once the water is boiling again, start the 5-minute timer.

3

Drain and butter

Drain the corn. Coat each ear with butter, then add salt, paprika, and optional pepper or chili powder.

4

Serve

Serve it hot next to the ribs and mac and cheese. Add one last small pinch of paprika if you want more color.

Best timing for your rib dinner

Start the corn water while the ribs are in their 425°F sauce finish. Add the corn when the water boils, and it will be ready right around the time the ribs rest.
