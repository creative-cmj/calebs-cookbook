Ready when you are. The timer pauses if you leave this app.

1

Heat and season

Heat oven to 275°F. Remove the thin membrane from the bone side if it is there. Mix the spices and rub half onto each rack.

2

Wrap tight

Put each rack meat-side up on its own big foil sheet. Add ¼ cup apple juice to each packet, then wrap tightly. Put both on a baking sheet.

3

Bake low and slow

Bake for 3 hours. The meat should look tender and pull back a little from the bones.

4

Sauce and finish

Carefully unwrap and brush on the first thin layer of BBQ sauce. Bake uncovered at 425°F for 6 minutes, add a second layer, then bake 6 more minutes until sticky.

5

Rest and slice

Rest 10 minutes. Slice between the bones and eat.

Hot-surface reminder

Open foil carefully because hot steam escapes fast. Use oven mitts for the pan, keep knife cuts between the bones, and check the ribs with a thermometer. Pork must reach at least 145°F, but ribs are usually tenderest around 190–203°F.

Quick food-safety reminder

Wash hands after touching raw meat. Keep raw-rib tools separate from cooked-rib tools. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
